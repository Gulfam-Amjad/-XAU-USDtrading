3. How We Turn Your Course Into a Trading System

An AI tool cannot simply "watch" a video and start trading, and it should not. We follow a careful, step-by-step process:

Course videos→ Transcripts + chart screenshots→ Strategy Rulebook→ Your approval→ Historical testing→ Demo trading→ Small live start
  1. Transcription and chart extraction. Every video is converted to text, and the key chart moments are captured as images.
  2. AI-assisted analysis. The transcripts and charts are analysed to identify every entry, exit and risk rule taught in the course.
  3. The Strategy Rulebook. A clear document that states each rule precisely, for example:
    "IF the daily trend is bullish AND price re-tests a weekly key level AND a bullish engulfing candle closes on the 15-minute chart, THEN open a BUY with the stop loss below the level."
  4. Your review and sign-off. You confirm the rules match how the course is meant to be traded. Nothing is built until you approve.

4. Your Control: Choose How the System Operates

You decide how much permission the system has, and you can switch modes at any time.

Level 1

Signal Only

The system finds trade opportunities and sends them to your phone with the reason, entry, stop loss and target. You place trades yourself.

Level 2

Semi-Automatic

The system prepares the trade and asks for permission. You tap "Approve" or "Reject", and it only trades with your approval.

Level 3

Fully Automatic (Within Limits)

The system trades by itself, but only inside the limits you set: risk per trade, daily loss, number of trades and trading hours.

Instructions you can give the system at any time

  • Pause / Resume trading.
  • Close all trades immediately (emergency stop).
  • Change mode (Signal / Semi-Auto / Full-Auto).
  • Adjust limits: risk per trade, daily loss limit, maximum trades per day.
  • Allow BUY only, SELL only, or both, for example if you have your own view on gold that week.
  • Block trading on specific days, for example around a major event.

5. Safety First: How Your Capital Is Protected

Many bots on the market blow up accounts because they use dangerous methods such as martingale (doubling the trade size after a loss) or grid trading (opening more and more trades against the market). Our system will never use these methods.

ProtectionWhat it does
Mandatory stop lossEvery trade is opened with a stop loss. No trade can exist without one.
Fixed risk per tradeEach trade risks only a small fixed percentage of the account, for example 0.5% - 1%, as you choose. Position size is calculated automatically.
Daily loss limitIf losses reach your daily limit (for example 2%), the system stops trading for the rest of the day.
Weekly / overall drawdown limitIf total losses reach a set level, the system stops completely and alerts you. Only you can restart it.
Maximum open tradesLimits how many trades can be open at once, to avoid over-exposure.
News filterAvoids trading around high-impact news (US jobs report, inflation data, Federal Reserve decisions) when gold moves violently.
Spread filterDoes not enter when the broker's spread is unusually wide, for example at market open or late at night.
Session filterTrades only during the sessions you approve, for example London and New York.
Connection safetyProtects against duplicate orders, platform restarts and internet disconnections.
Emergency stopOne command from your phone closes all trades and stops the system.
Prop-firm mode (optional)If you trade a funded account (for example FTMO-style), the system respects that firm's drawdown rules automatically.
What this means in practice: losses are a normal part of every trading strategy, and no system can avoid them completely. But with these limits, no single trade and no single bad day can cause serious damage to your account. The maximum risk is defined in advance and agreed by you.

7. How We Prove Reliability Before Any Real Money Is Used

You asked how reliable the system's decisions will be. Our answer: we measure it rather than promise it. The system is tested in stages, and you see the results of each stage before we move to the next.

StageWhat happensWhat you receive
1. Historical testingThe rules are tested on several years of real gold price data, including realistic spreads, commissions and slippage.Performance report
2. Unseen-data testThe rules are tested on a period of data that was not used to build them, to make sure the results are genuine and not "curve-fitted".Validation report
3. Stress testThousands of simulated scenarios estimate realistic worst-case losing streaks.Worst-case risk figures
4. Demo accountThe system trades live market conditions with virtual money for several weeks.Daily reports + demo summary
5. Small live startReal trading starts with minimum risk and increases only if results match expectations and you approve.Daily / weekly reports

Key numbers you will see in every report

  • Win rate: percentage of winning trades
  • Profit factor: total profit divided by total loss (above 1.3 is generally considered healthy)
  • Risk-to-reward ratio: average win compared to average loss
  • Maximum drawdown: the largest fall from a peak, the most important safety number
An honest advantage: even the first testing stage is valuable on its own. It shows you objectively whether the course strategy has a real statistical edge on gold before you risk any money on it. Most bot sellers never offer this.
